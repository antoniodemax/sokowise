# SokoWise Real-World Test

Thank you for trying SokoWise. This page tells you what it is, what to try, and how to tell us what you think.

**Test website:** https://sokowise-staging.vercel.app

## What SokoWise is

SokoWise is a simple app for small businesses in Kenya. You record what you sell, what you paid for stock, what customers owe you (deni) and what you spend. It then shows you, on your phone, how much you sold, how much money you actually have, who owes you, and what is running low. It works with cash, M-Pesa and credit.

## Who should test

- Shop and duka owners
- Boutique and cosmetics sellers
- Salon and barbershop owners
- Food businesses (kiosks, small hotels, mama fua, caterers)
- Electronics and phone accessory shops
- Any other small business that keeps records in a book today

You do not need to know accounting. If something in the app does not make sense to you, that is useful for us to hear.

## Important: this is a test version

- This is a **test website**, not the final product. Please **do not enter sensitive personal information or your real, important financial records**. Use it the way you would use a rough book to try things out.
- Data may be reset while we test. Do not rely on it as your only record.
- The first time you open the app after a while, it can take **up to a minute** to wake up. This is a limit of the free test server, not your phone. Please wait and try again if the first page is slow.
- The **Copilot** (the "ask a question" helper) may say it is not available. That part needs paid credits that are not always on during the test.
- **Receipt photos** may disappear from the app after we update the test server. Everything you typed in stays.
- M-Pesa messages you paste stay inside your own business account. Nothing is sent to Safaricom, and SokoWise does not check with Safaricom.

## What to try

Use your phone. Register with a phone number you can remember. It takes about a minute.

1. **Create an account.** Open the website, tap **Get started**, enter your name, phone number, a password and your business name. Choose your type of business.
2. **Add some products.** The dashboard shows "What do you sell?". Tick the items you sell from the list, fix the prices, and add them. Or skip the list and tap **Products → Add product** to add your own.
3. **Add starting stock.** When you add a product, type how many you have in the shop now ("Stock now"). You can also do it later under **Inventory → Opening stock**.
4. **Record a sale.** Tap **New sale**, search an item, tap it, and record.
5. **Try cash.** Pay by cash and record it.
6. **Try M-Pesa.** Choose M-Pesa as the payment and type the M-Pesa code if you have one.
7. **Try credit (deni).** Choose Credit. The app will ask you to pick a customer.
8. **Add a customer.** Under **Customers**, add someone who buys on credit. Then sell to them on credit and watch what they owe.
9. **Record a repayment.** Open the customer and tap **Record repayment** when they pay you back.
10. **Add an expense.** Under **Expenses**, record something you paid for (rent, transport, airtime).
11. **Check your dashboard.** Look at Sold, In your pocket, Deni, and Profit. Do the numbers make sense to you?
12. **Check inventory.** See what is running low, and add stock with **Restock**.
13. **Try analytics.** Look at the week and the month. Is this useful?
14. **Try the Copilot, if it is available.** Ask a question like "How much did I sell today?" or "Who owes me money?".
15. **Try receipt scanning, if it is available.** Under **Inventory → Scan receipt**, photograph a supplier receipt.
16. **Try the M-Pesa page.** Under **M-Pesa**, paste a confirmation SMS and see whether the app matches it to a sale. Use a test message, not a real customer's details.

If you have a helper in the shop, the owner can add them under **Settings → Add staff**. Staff can record sales and repayments but cannot see profit, expenses or change settings.

## Feedback questions

You can answer these in a WhatsApp message, a call, or on paper. Short answers are fine.

1. What type of business do you run?
2. What did you try to do?
3. What was confusing?
4. What did you like?
5. What did you expect to find but could not?
6. What would make you use this every day?
7. Did anything break or show an error? What were you doing when it happened?
8. What should SokoWise do next?

There is a longer template in `TESTER_FEEDBACK_TEMPLATE.md` if you prefer a form.

## If something goes wrong

- If a page looks stuck, wait a minute (the server may be waking up), then pull down to refresh.
- If you are signed out, sign in again with your phone number and password.
- If you forget your password, tell us. There is no "forgot password" link yet in this test version.
- Tell us what you were doing and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
